# Targeted related-work assessment

Checked 2026-09-24. This is a narrow primary-source search, not a novelty
certification or exhaustive literature review. Web search publication-date
snippets were not used as publication dates.

1. **Trade obstructions are established.** Alan Taylor and William Zwicker,
   *A Characterization of Weighted Voting*, Proceedings of the American
   Mathematical Society 115(4):1089–1094, 1992,
   [original paper](https://www.math.hkust.edu.hk/~maykwok/courses/MATH392K/07Spring/trade%20robust.pdf),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1992-1092927-0).
   Their trading characterization directly precedes the two-trade inequality
   used here. Calling the four-configuration obstruction novel would be wrong.
   Our positive-budget orientation reverses the usual winning-coalition
   convention; the incidence-sum argument is the same.

2. **Intersections of weighted rules and dimension are established.**
   Taylor and Zwicker, *Weighted Voting, Multicameral Representation, and
   Power*, Games and Economic Behavior 5(1):170–181, 1993,
   [publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0899825683710092),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/game.1993.1009).
   Moving from one additive threshold to an intersection of two is not by
   itself a contribution. The semantic rows are contained in unrestricted
   generic rows of the same count. The finite comparison must test selection
   or estimation performance, not assert an impossible class separation.

3. **The concentration bound is established.** Wassily Hoeffding,
   *Probability Inequalities for Sums of Bounded Random Variables*, Journal
   of the American Statistical Association 58(301):13–30, 1963,
   [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830).
   Proposition 2 uses the familiar bounded-sample inequality and a union bound.
   Publisher text access failed in this environment; no direct quotation is
   used and the inequality is derived as the standard named tool, with every
   statistical assumption stated. It is not claimed as an original bound.

4. **Measurement-to-decision transfer has stronger precedents.** Tanner
   Fiez, Lalit Jain, Kevin G. Jamieson, and Lillian Ratliff,
   *Sequential Experimental Design for Transductive Linear Bandits*,
   NeurIPS 2019,
   [proceedings](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/8ba6c657b03fc7c8dd4dff8e45defcd2-Abstract.html),
   [paper](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/9251-sequential-experimental-design-for-transductive-linear-bandits.pdf).
   They distinguish measurable vectors from target decision vectors and
   develop adaptive experimental design with instance-dependent bounds.
   An interaction-coefficient probe model is a specialized instance of that
   broad statistical framework, not a new observation model in itself.

5. **Combinatorial pure exploration is established.** Tongyi Cao and
   Akshay Krishnamurthy, *Disagreement-Based Combinatorial Pure Exploration:
   Sample Complexity Bounds and an Efficient Algorithm*, COLT 2019,
   PMLR 99:558–588,
   [proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v99/cao19a.html).
   Their adaptive algorithms and structural sample-complexity analysis show
   why a generic uniform-sampling comparison is not a compelling statistical
   baseline. No claim of an improved exploration algorithm is made here.

The candidate contribution is the explicitly sequential conjunction of
power, genuinely different competitive rewards, retained source use, full
historical compatibility, and bounded portfolios in a fixed interaction
model. The current construction proves one finite example of that conjunction;
it does not establish a new general characterization. **Originality and
AISTATS-level contribution remain UNESTABLISHED.** Real event-derived
structure, a nontrivial generalization, and comparable empirical evidence
are still needed before claiming the intended paper contribution.
