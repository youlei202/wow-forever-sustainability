The numerical capacity maxima use an additional elementary calculation on the
same fitted one-dimensional line, not a replacement for R5. The old profile's
delta-ball intersects the line in a suffix. All new/new distances are exactly
beta times their parameter distance. A prefix of length ell contains at most
floor(beta*ell/delta)+1 separated points; the frozen robust construction reaches
that numeric count. This is a model-specific finite capacity calculation,
not a new packing theorem or native population-optimality certificate.

The sufficient conditions could be generalized to a globally co-Lipschitz
observable map, but this round does not need or claim such an extension.
R6 uses the supplied affine theorem as written. The positive result does not
require a nullspace of every primitive response: an embedded utility-neutral
line inside the inequality region is enough. Distinct damage channels can
vary while total DPS stays below an unchanged old anchor.

Only total task DPS is capped and proven neutral here. Fixed resource/event
controls are audited, but damage-channel maxima themselves increase in some
directions, as intended by the behavior design. Consequently this is not the
R5 zero-growth-in-every-capability strengthening when those channels are
declared separate capability endpoints. The capability ontology must be
specified before making broader power-neutrality claims.

The shared structural interpretation remains conditional: frontier-neutral
behavior space can avoid new legacy obligations; frontier-moving repair may
create such obligations in R5's restricted model. R6 establishes the native
positive mechanism in its narrow stress domain, and records why the attempted
native negative mechanism did not instantiate its premises.
