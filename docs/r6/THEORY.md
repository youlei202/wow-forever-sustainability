## Theorem 1: exact repair closure requires exact native associations

The native instance must have one task, a fixed raw relevance allowance
epsilon, unchanged protected H, old frontier f0 and source values
`f0-epsilon <= v_i <= f0`. The cap obeys `f0 <= tau <= f0+epsilon`.
The mandatory novelty core has q at least f0. Both q and every designated
repair performance p_i lie in `[tau-epsilon,tau]`.

The decisive native restrictions are the **equalities**, not merely the item
names or intended associations:

```
V_i(R) = max(v_i,p_i) if i is repaired, otherwise v_i
frontier(R) = max(f0,q,all activated p_i).
```

Every legal off-diagonal coequipment, every allowed policy, and every
core-containing configuration must be included when checking these equalities.
A core may itself restore a protected source if the two can coequip. A repair
nominally assigned to source j can restore another source i. Either effect
invalidates the unique-repair response model. A designated diagonal table
cannot establish it while ignoring stronger legal alternatives.

When the full response equalities and stable near-cap assumptions hold, R5's
closure uses strict comparison `v_i+epsilon < current_frontier`; equality is
competitive. The least closure computes the exact minimum completion of that
specified core, including the core in the release count. The intermediate
closure steps are planning states and can fail L. They are not successful
published updates. To interpret this as the minimum of **any** novel release,
the core must additionally be the only qualifying novelty route.

R5's source-selective model also makes N, P and a size-one old portfolio
automatic, but native data still must establish its actual observable D and
unchanged H. If only the scalar closure recurrence is computed, these other
targets remain separately checked or `not_established`. Multiple tasks with
alternative source niches cannot be reduced silently to one threshold.

The matched local/cascade construction in R5 has the same H, core, cap and
direct associations. Its final movements are eta/(2m) and eta, respectively.
Both can be arbitrarily small in absolute terms; the theorem does not match
their final frontiers exactly. This distinction matters when interpreting the
R6 request for comparable power movement.

## Why scalar reward tuning may be an assumption boundary

Native scalar amplitude aliases sharing a fixed control kernel often satisfy
`u_i(theta)=a_i*theta+b_i` with nonnegative a_i for every old partner and policy.
The largest amplitude then weakly improves every legal source-partner loadout.
This is a direct audit of R5's no-alternative-repair hypothesis: a larger repair
is not confined to the label of its designated old source. The same issue can
arise at the core when it improves source-containing configurations.

For a finite full opening, if replacing each weaker repair by the strongest
preserves physical legality and weakly dominates utility in every context,
the strongest repair can reproduce all repair-side source usefulness and
portfolio witnesses. A retained core's unchanged D witness remains available
when the reduced opening has no higher frontier. This is a conditional reason
to expect collapse to a small completion, not an additional R5 theorem or an
assumption that native D automatically holds. Arbitrary subset admission,
different control kernels, incompatible replacements, alternative D witnesses
or nonmonotone response rows need their own analysis.

The bounded native gate search therefore asks whether actual set thresholds
or weapon restrictions create source-selective effects after all off-diagonal
loadouts are retained. A native effect gate is acceptable evidence; a new
source-label-dependent bonus is not. If no supported gate meets the exact
response model, the scalar full-cross experiment is reported as a violated
assumption boundary, not a fabricated cascade or a disproof of R5.

## Theorem 3: exact conditions for the frontier-neutral sequence

The fixed interface expands one slot only. Every new design is an alternative
in that slot; no two new designs can be equipped together. The complete finite
old partner set J, allowed policy set Pi and tasks remain fixed. All original
H configurations, physics, caps, source obligations and old portfolio stay
available and initially feasible.

On the entire declared parameter domain, primitive **mean** utilities and
tracked capabilities must be affine. One fixed partner/policy/task is the
usefulness and novelty witness. Its task weight must reach the required N,
D and L masses, and its usefulness allowance is the minimum of the raw N/L
tolerances. Crucially, R5 also requires its combat-observable behavior map
`phi(theta)=B theta+b` to be affine. Item identifiers or artificial parameter
coordinates do not satisfy this requirement.

The polytope uses all utility rows bounded by the **old frontier**, not merely
the looser cap, and a witness utility lower bound. Novelty is not used to fit
the polytope. Nonutility capabilities use their declared caps; if the claimed
result is zero growth in every tracked coordinate, they must instead be
bounded by their own initial maxima.

An intrinsic cube `theta_c+V[-h,h]^r` must be contained in that region. Each
linear constraint is checked using
`a^T theta_c+h*||V^T a||_1 <= bound`. Its behavioral map must satisfy the
global gain inequality `||B V z||_inf >= beta ||z||_inf` with positive beta.
R5's full-rank and singular-value arguments apply because B is linear. A
local rank estimate or Jacobian singular value of an arbitrary nonlinear map
does not supply this certificate.

R3's normalized damage shares are ratios of affine responses over its full
amplitude rectangle. Hence affine utility calibration alone does not make
that rectangle an instance of Theorem3. A constant-total-utility affine slice
can make the same physical damage shares affine by fixing their denominator,
provided native event/resource controls remain unchanged. Such a slice is a
valid embedded cube if its containment and gain are actually verified. These
are conditions to test, not a declaration that a proposed native slice works.

## M0, p, geometry and rounding

M0 counts all old-only and historical profiles at the fixed witness task,
including every allowed old partner/policy combination. Profiles can be
deduplicated only when they are actually identical under the declared metric.
For each accepted item, `p=|J|*|Pi|` bounds **all** new profiles that become old
comparators next round, not just its winning or competitive profile. It
includes the accepted witness itself, so p is at least one. Fixed slot
alternatives keep p constant. If previously introduced designs can coequip
across other expanding slots, their extra profiles must be counted; the
fixed-interface theorem cannot be applied unchanged.

For positive delta and beta, grid spacing `3*delta/beta` inside each intrinsic
axis of length2h gives `L=1+floor(2h*beta/(3*delta))` coordinates and `N=L^r`
points. Their designated behaviors are separated by at least3delta. One
delta-ball around an archive profile blocks at most one grid point, including
at equality under the conservative closed-ball removal convention.

After k accepted items, at most `M0+k*p` grid points have been removed. Thus
the next point exists while this quantity is less than N. This yields exactly
the stated lower guarantee `ceil(max(0,N-M0)/p)`: if N-M0 is divisible by p,
there is no extra guaranteed update at equality. Initial point counts larger
than N give zero, not a negative capacity. A zero certificate is not an
impossibility result. The geometry, interface and full archive counts must be
established before interpreting this arithmetic as a native guarantee.

## Interval closure and implementation checks

R5 section6.1 assumes fixed f0 and simultaneous intervals for thresholds,
core and repairs. The forced closure uses upper threshold endpoints with
lower core/repair endpoints. The possible closure uses lower thresholds with
upper core/repair endpoints. Their least fixed points bracket the true closure
**conditional on the response-model assumptions**. Agreement identifies the
repair set; disagreement is unresolved. It does not justify discarding unsafe
old H, invalid near-cap witnesses or legal alternative repairs.

`src/wowfs/experiments/r6_theory.py` implements these supplied recurrences and
checks. Its tests cover the supplied four-source cascade/local construction,
strict equality, every endpoint realization of a small interval system,
core/off-diagonal alternative restoration, independent stable-witness
validation, and the grid formula's divisible/remainder cases. These are
arithmetic/implementation checks, not native experiments or new proofs of
originality.

## Native A outcome: two real gates, no constructed cascade

The frozen probe used Human Warrior, high armor, one fixed policy, 2,048
matched seeds and the entire four-hand by three-leg equipment matrix. The old
hands were 14551, 21998 and 21278; core hands were 19143. Legs were old 22385
or repairs 22000 and 15057. Two fixed pieces of Heroism and two of Stormshroud
made the respective matching source/repair combinations activate native
four-piece effects. All off-diagonal and anchor/core combinations were kept.
Two additional same-equipment controls disabled only the matching four-piece
callback, for 14 native calls and 28,672 battles total.

The Heroism and Stormshroud gate effects were respectively +3.1926 DPS
(approximate simultaneous 95% interval [2.7632, 3.6220]) and +1.0674 DPS
([1.0644, 1.0704]); the family comprises 14 marginal means and these two paired
contrasts. These are actual effects, but their gains do not offset the static
cost of the leg replacements. The old frontier was 175.6577 DPS, the core
value 173.7891, and the two designated repair values 169.3525 and 165.4766.
Consequently q is below f0, and the core/repairs fail R5's stable band
[f0, 1.05*f0]. No protected old source becomes threatened.

All four core-required release subsets were exhaustively evaluated in the
complete legal matrix. At the finite-table point estimates they pass P/N/L/H/C
and fail D. There is no jointly feasible completed release in this declared
domain. The formal predicted minimum is therefore **not applicable**; a scalar
closure projection returning an empty repair set is not a native validation of
Theorem 1. The larger m=4/8 gated instances and matched local/cascade statistical
experiment are `not_run`, because their prerequisites were not constructed.
No epsilon, item-stat or admission retuning followed this result.

## Native B/C interpretation and independent analysis review

The original R3 rectangle diagnostic gives a zero R5 lower bound at delta=.05
when all four partners and three policies are counted: M0=p=12 at the witness
task. This is a zero certificate, not a proof of zero native capacity.

The separate amplitude stress domain fixes one old partner, policy and task,
so M0=p=1. The old research weapon has DPS180/tick0; new research amplitudes
are DPS[1,175], tick[0,500]. These are not official catalogue balance values.
The frozen behavior is five native action-channel DPS coordinates divided by
the fixed initial calibration scalar F0. This differs from R3's own-total
damage fractions. It measures reward composition; fixed native controls do
not establish changes in action selection or gameplay strategy. Only total
task DPS is capped; the channels are not individually capped capabilities.

The calibration's affine model and embedded constant-utility segment give
R5 lower bound 1 at delta=.05. The first frozen sequence passed independent
confirmation. Four subsequent sequences use the identical segment and
predeclared thresholds .025/.035/.05/.075. Their fitted-line exact numerical
capacities are 10/7/5/3; R5 lower bounds are 3/2/1/1. For this one-dimensional
affine image, candidate distance is beta times parameter separation. The
initial archive excludes a suffix, so packing its remaining prefix gives the
stated numerical maximum. This is not a whole-game or population upper bound.

Final confirmation uses only 32 new disjoint blocks of 2,048 seeds per design,
with no pooling, refitting or changed sequence. All four complete sequences
pass the approximate simultaneous checks; minimum novelty lower bounds are
.0258711/.0365437/.0515443/.0812484, respectively. They are four alternative
sequences, not a combined 25-item history. The initial smaller C panel had two
unresolved novelty bounds and partially overlapped B's seed ranges; it remains
archived and is not represented as fully independent confirmation.

Independent source review checked full-prefix history comparisons, paired
seed ordering, block axes, degrees of freedom and Bonferroni family counts.
Utility and behavior receive half of each panel's error budget, and the four
panels share total alpha=.05 by a second union bound. Utility uses per-seed
paired contrasts; behavior uses paired block contrasts with 31 degrees of
freedom in final C. Regression fixtures test common block-noise cancellation,
nonshared uncertainty and a candidate close to a nonadjacent historical
profile. These tests are synthetic software checks, not additional native
evidence.

The fixed calibration F0 and the unknown population old frontier remain
distinct. Confirmation checks both absolute new utility against frozen F0
and paired new-minus-fresh-old utility against zero. Student-t intervals are
approximate for native Monte Carlo outputs, including block means; finite
held-out affinity checks do not prove an exact population affine identity.
The R5 theorem is exact conditional on its model, while these native results
provide finite-domain, approximate inferential support for its premises and
selected constructions.
