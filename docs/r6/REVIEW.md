# R6 — Theory-to-native critical experiments

**Result:** a native frontier-neutral research family supports repeated useful,
behaviorally distinct releases. The attempted native repair cascade did not
meet the supplied R5 assumptions. The two-sided foundational claim is therefore
not established.

1. **Did native combat exhibit an endogenous repair cascade?** No. The bounded
   two-source gate probe found real Heroism and Stormshroud bonuses, but the
   proposed core threatened zero sources and the repair loadouts were too weak.
2. **Were matched local/cascading updates constructed under one cap?** No.
   All four core-required release subsets were enumerated; all failed D.
   The R5 minimum-release prediction is not applicable to this probe.
3. **Was there a nonempty frontier-neutral region?** Yes, in an explicitly
   restricted native research domain: one Human Warrior, one 180-second
   high-armor task, one policy, one fixed partner loadout, and one expanding slot.
4. **How many repeated useful/distinct updates kept the frontier fixed?** Five
   at delta=0.05. Across the four predeclared thresholds, the final independently
   confirmed sequence lengths were 10, 7, 5 and 3. These are alternative sequences,
   not 25 consecutive updates. The old portfolio stays K=1.
5. **Did the theorem predict the outcome?** The frozen primary R5 grid bound
   guaranteed one update and its one-update sequence passed fresh confirmation.
   In the unchanged-region capacity study, the R5 lower bounds were 3, 2, 1, 1;
   confirmed counts exceeded each. Theorems 1, 2 and 4 were not instantiated
   natively. Population-level exact affine identities remain model assumptions.
6. **Which assumption mattered most?** For A, stable near-cap repair witnesses
   and a genuinely novel, frontier-moving core were missing. For B, the fixed
   one-slot interface and measurable damage-channel freedom inside a useful
   region kept both the frontier and future archive cost under control.
7. **Is this the complete foundational paper core?** Not yet. It is a native
   positive-half instantiation and an informative negative construction attempt.
   The stress amplitudes and narrow context limit application breadth; the
   repair-cascade half and statistical locality remain native open questions.

## A: actual native gates, unsuccessful cascade construction

The frozen probe contains every combination of four gloves and three legs,
plus two same-gear set-bonus-off controls. Heroism 4pc adds 10-rage proc
feedback; Stormshroud 4pc adds 14 AP. Their paired native DPS gains were
3.1926 and 1.0674. Approximate simultaneous intervals for the declared family
were [2.7632, 3.6220] and [1.06436, 1.07039].

However, replacing the old legs loses more static performance than either
bonus restores. The old frontier was 175.6577 DPS and cap 184.4406. The core
reached only 173.7891; designated repairs reached 169.3525 and 165.4766,
below their sources' old 172.4177 and 171.9133. Initial protection was valid,
but q>=f0 and the stable witness band [f0,1.05 f0] failed. All four releases
retained P/N/L/H/C and failed D. No after-the-fact stat tuning, tolerance
reduction or off-diagonal blacklist was used.

The response association audit happened to pass in this weak-repair table:
no off-diagonal or core configuration improved a protected source. That
does not repair the failed stable-witness and novelty assumptions. The empty
scalar closure and its interval bracket are recorded only as conditional
projections, not as a successful native theorem test. m=4 and m=8 are
`not_run`, because this bounded search supplied no corresponding independent
native gate family. Experiment D is `not_run` for lack of a valid A pair.

## B: useful native behavior changes beneath a fixed frontier

First, the existing R3 rectangle was inspected without rerunning or altering
its caches. Human Warrior, speed 1.3, all four declared offhand/trinket
partners, three policies and four tasks give 48 context responses. On an
explicit useful constant-utility line, beta=0.0224117 and M0=p=12; at
delta=0.05 its R5 capacity bound is zero. This is a zero certificate for that
line, not an impossibility result for the whole rectangle or game.

The separate stress family was frozen before new native calibration:

- Fixed high-armor task (10,000 armor), 180 seconds, Human Warrior and
  `native_reck`; Thunderfury offhand and Hand of Justice/Blackhand's Breadth.
- Blood Talon main-hand alternatives, fixed speed 1.3, PPM and tick schedule.
  Old research anchor: physical DPS 180, periodic tick damage 0. New domain:
  physical DPS [1,175], tick damage [0,500]. These amplitudes **exceed the
  original R3 range** and do not describe an official equipment balance patch.
- Behavior: five native action-channel DPS coordinates divided by the frozen
  initial frontier. The normalization is fixed, unlike R3's per-design damage
  fractions. No item identity or parameter label is a behavior coordinate.
- Total task DPS is the tracked capability. Damage channels are distinct
  behavior directions, not separately capped capabilities. This does not
  assert zero growth in healing, survival, PvP or unmeasured capabilities.

Three 2,048-seed anchors fit affine utility/action responses; an independently
specified fourth point was held out. Its largest per-seed DPS residual was
1.14e-13. Aggregate controls and normalized first-seed event traces matched.
The samplewise checks support the fixed-control mechanism; finite checks do
not prove every population response over a continuous domain.

A final descriptive structural audit checks all 736 unique precision-panel
cells. Controls match within every block. Three post-hoc response anchors per
block predict 640 held-out cells / 1,310,720 per-seed observations with maximum
DPS residual 2.84e-13; five action-channel mean residuals are at most4.27e-13.
This audit changes no frozen coefficients, admission decisions or sequence.
It checks aggregate controls; no per-seed event-trace identity is inferred.

The fitted old frontier was 254.6379938 DPS. Six primitive inequalities bound
the new amplitudes and place utility between 95% and 100% of that frontier.
The frozen embedded line is at 97.5%, from (1,435.6241) to (166.8114,0).
Its certificate has r=1, h=0.5, beta=0.2499374, M0=p=1. At delta=0.05,
the R5 grid has two points and guarantees one accepted design after initial
archive interference. That selected design passed its separate 8,192-seed
confirmation without retuning.

One expanding slot makes every research design mutually exclusive with all
other designs in that slot. All declared partner/policy combinations are
included—there is exactly one in this stress experiment. Fixed old equipment
remains usable. The fixed admission branch has six inequalities plus an
unchanged legacy branch; the item catalogue and archive themselves grow.

The region and four thresholds were frozen before primary sequence outcomes.
C was activated after B passed. A one-dimensional interval packing calculation
then selected each sequence before its native evaluations. Its numeric maximum
concerns the fitted line and designated behavior map, not native population
optimality or unrestricted equipment expansion. Four points are mechanism
evidence, not an empirical asymptotic scaling law. Dimension scaling is
`not_run`; no second certified direction was manufactured.

The first capacity panel passed every point-estimate check, but two thresholds
had unresolved novelty confidence bounds. All parameters and order were kept
fixed for one final, fixed-size independent precision panel: 32 blocks of
2,048 seeds per distinct design, or 65,536 samples. No initial-panel data was
pooled into final inference. Utility uses paired per-seed contrasts; behavior
uses paired block-level channel contrasts. Two-sided Student-t intervals use
explicit family counts, splitting each panel's alpha=0.0125 between utility
and behavior. The union allocation across all four panels is 0.05. Coverage
is approximate, not distribution-free or an exact finite-sample guarantee.

At delta=0.05, all five updates pass the complete checks. The smallest
simultaneous novelty lower bound is 0.0515443; the smallest certified margin
below the old frontier is 5.0852 DPS. Every old/new source retains a useful
loadout, and the unchanged old loadout covers the only task with K=1.
All historical profiles are included in each comparison. The global frontier
equals the retained old frontier at every observed prefix.

## Execution, audit and limits

R6 executed **1,140 native calls / 1,753,088 physical battles**, with zero
failed or incomplete invocations and zero independent audit errors. All six
run archives were checked against frozen sources, binaries, inputs, imported
R5 hash, cache keys and raw outputs. Coverage is Human Warrior only.

There are 1,746,944 distinct exact combat-design/seed observations. The initial
C panel partially reused B seed ranges, causing 6,144 repeated design/seed
iterations. A and B also shared 1,024 seed values across different designs.
These physical runs remain counted, but the stages are not claimed independent.
The final precision panel has zero seed-range overlap with any earlier R6
stage. `SEED_OVERLAP.json` preserves both denominators and the overlap audit.

The maintained Python suite passed 86 tests. No old frozen run was modified;
no remote push, public submission, fabricated class coverage or originality
claim was made. The ordinary native simulator remains a pinned community
engine, not validation of live-server gameplay or player satisfaction.

The result supports a limited shared interpretation: a fixed useful region
with real behavior variation can avoid inducing legacy obligations. It does
not yet show that native frontier movement produces the particular R5 repair
cascade, nor that this dichotomy explains the whole game.
