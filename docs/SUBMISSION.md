## Title

Progression without Power Creep: Sustainable Expansion of Equipment Ecosystems

## Abstract draft

Sequential reward systems must introduce useful choices while retaining the
competitive uses of earlier rewards. Individually balanced additions can still
violate a fixed performance envelope when their effects combine. We formulate
sustainable equipment expansion as sequential constrained decision making with
requirements for power, reward usefulness, behavioral distinction, legacy use,
historical compatibility, and bounded portfolio size. We study how the geometry
of legal configurations limits admission mechanisms, separating additive price
rules from rules that expose interaction structure. Small finite constructions
and exhaustive diagnostics connect compatibility constraints to failures of
scalar pricing and identify what a structured restriction must preserve. We
separate finite-model feasibility, stochastic confirmation, and performance in
a native simulator. World of Warcraft: Forever motivates the application and
defines a target evaluation across its legal race--class contexts. The current
evidence is limited to mathematical constructions and abstract finite models;
native simulator evaluation remains unavailable. The study provides a precise
basis for testing sustainable expansion without interpreting missing results as
success or claiming unlimited novelty in a finite behavior space.

This draft makes no numerical empirical claim and is not submission-ready. The
abstract must be updated if the eventual evidence or scientific scope changes.
