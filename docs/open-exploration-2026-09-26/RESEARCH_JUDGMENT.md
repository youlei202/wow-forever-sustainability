# Research judgment after open exploration

Status: updated after frozen confirmation, 26 September 2026. The `confirm-v2`
native batch completed 960 physical inputs and 72,351,744 battles with zero
failed inputs. Seven of eight setting-level claims meet every frozen
condition. The failed Mage held-out claim is a supported safe joint update,
not just an unresolved interval. These are four local/held-out pairs, not seven
independent discoveries. Development and confirmation evidence are explicitly
separated below. No manuscript has been revised.

The strongest candidate paper core is the confirmed actual-item Trinket
continuation result, organized around this question: **what information about retained
sources and legal compositions must an update interface preserve so that a
good present reward does not destroy useful future updates?** The experiments
make this a concrete design issue. They do not establish a new planning
algorithm, an unknown combat mechanism, or published originality. A paper
centered on large-scale superiority over simple greedy release planning is
not supported by the challenges.

## What changed the research judgment

The decisive negative result is the strength of ordinary reward staging.
Minimum-qualified-increment methods eliminate all 46 broad and all 17 original
catalog planning advantages, and 144 of 153 refinement advantages. A full-menu
challenge of twelve residual refinement/two-slot rows leaves six count gaps;
in five, a simple method reaches or dominates the exact plan's terminal
frontier. More release dates mostly meant distributing an available gain more
slowly. The remaining sixth is an N=256 development result with a small terminal
difference, not a confirmed algorithmic win.

Restoring candidate alternatives also changes the story. The apparent Shaman
coordination advantage vanishes in both challenged sixteen-candidate worlds:
retaining capacity is two for widths one, two and four. A finite-domain upper
bound cannot be carried over from a menu with omitted repairs. The Orc Warrior
hit example still has mean-table capacities zero, one and one, respectively,
but was not selected as an independent confirmed finding in this campaign.

These are substantive rejections of attractive explanations. They are not
reasons to discard the underlying source-retention question. They require a
more specific target than “planning gets more updates.”

## Recommended core: preserving future source witnesses

The best development example uses actual catalog items, fixed native policies,
two tasks, two growing slots, every physical cross, and all fourteen unpublished
candidates. The initial pair is Eye of the Beast (13968, trinket) and Drakestone
(10796, off hand). Fix gain `.01`, headroom `.03`, retention tolerance `.01`,
equal task weights, task mass `.5`, and total publication budget twelve. Task
vectors below are **[180-second long task, 30-second short task]**.

| First choice, N=4096 development | Current frontier | Current source task masses | Remaining retaining / value capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Second Wind (11819, a07) | `[1.026421816, 1]` | Eye `.5`, Second Wind `1`, Drakestone `1` | `0 / 1` |
| Spellbound Tome (18695, x3) | `[1.025966351, 1]` | Eye `1`, Drakestone `.5`, Tome `1` | `1 / 1` |

Both widths one and two give these exact finite **mean-table** counts with the
same total item budget. The maximum current-frontier difference is `.000455466`
of the reference. The minimum source mass, total source mass and unlabeled
multiset `[.5,1,1]` also match. The vulnerable source identity does not match.
This is a near-match, not exact equality of native response functions or of
the labeled source-response state.

Tome followed by Draconic Infused Emblem (22268, a03) reaches
`[1.028700211,1.027406595]`; Eye remains useful through Eye+Tome on the long
task. After Second Wind, Emblem has value gain but makes Eye useless on both
tasks. Tome is still in the candidate pool, yet its combination with Second
Wind reaches `1.045659796`, above cap `1.03`. Adding more items cannot remove
that immutable legal combination. Every singleton/pair continuation is checked;
this is a complete finite catalog obstruction, not a cherry-picked failed
repair. The good path's narrowest prominent cap slack is about `.00130`, which
is why confidence-supported bounds are necessary.

Independent confirmation supports the full claim in both the original and
one unseen duration-transformed setting: retaining continuation bounds are
exactly `[0,0]` versus `[1,1]`, and value-only bounds `[1,1]` on both sides,
for B=1 and B=2. Every first-step source mass in the table is also closed at
the stated value by the simultaneous bounds. The first-frontier differences
are supported within `.0025` on both tasks. The local confirmation means are
`[1.024335699,1]` versus `[1.024274819,1]`; the held-out means are
`[1.022860041,1]` versus `[1.023748388,1]`. The slight DPS ordering therefore
does not itself transfer. Neither case is an exact-response equality result.

The local good path's terminal power-margin lower bounds are positive on both
tasks, `[0.680600,1.001429]` raw DPS. Its Eye long-task retention interval is
strictly positive. Conversely, the frozen blocked-repair path has long-task
cap-margin interval `[-4.070412,-3.695870]` DPS locally and
`[-3.496027,-3.138224]` DPS in the held-out setting. The unwanted composition
cannot be made safe by publishing additional immutable items. The overall
zero remaining capacity is certified only for the specified B=1 and B=2;
these checks do not exclude every conceivable larger batch or unmeasured item.

An independent check from compact means/covariance, without importing the
campaign solver or inference code, reproduces the bad-side finite upper
bounds. It checks all 91 singleton/pair continuations in each Trinket setting
and all 120 first plus 756 second continuation edges in each Magister setting.
The unchosen first alternative remains present. Forty-eight supported path
prefixes also pass complete source/incidence checks; numerical margin
differences are at most `1.02e-13` DPS. The certificate is in
`checks/SECONDARY_CATALOG_CAPACITY_CHECK.json`.

This can change the choice between the two first rewards. It does **not** imply
that every current-DPS method chooses badly: full-domain equal-weight max-gain
already succeeds by publishing Emblem then Tome; existing two-step lookahead
chooses Tome then Emblem. The tested source-aware worst-margin rule chooses
Second Wind and gets stuck, while some minimum-increment weightings succeed.
An effective simple remedy is part of the evidence, not a competing result to
hide. The claim is about the information used for the specified comparison,
not a universal failure of greedy algorithms.

The actual Magister catalog gives a second, weaker version. At N=4096,
`g=.01,h=.1,e=.005`, the Arcanist Gloves versus Sorcerer's Gloves first choices
have retained continuation one versus two, and value-only three versus three,
for both widths and all sixteen candidates. Confirmation closes these same
bounds locally and in the held-out setting. Its source coverage visibly
differs: Sorcerer's Gloves cover both tasks initially, while Arcanist Gloves
cover only the long task at this tolerance. In development Sorcerer's Gloves
also had slightly better current performance on both tasks; confirmation
instead has a task trade-off. Source coverage remains a simple explanation,
and the example establishes no general algorithm superiority. Moreover, the original `e=.01` claim **failed** the higher
precision development challenge: both retaining continuations became two.
The frozen `.005` hypothesis is a newly selected development threshold.

The two catalog cases should be treated as examples of one research question,
not two independent new mechanisms. They extend the motivation for the supplied
structural note to a finite multitask, newly co-equipped catalog setting. They
do not reproduce that note's exact functional-equality theorem, prove R5's
arbitrarily long repair cascade, or establish novelty over richer existing
source/quality-resource interfaces.

## Supporting result: safe parts do not certify safe composition

Two research stat-variant worlds give a separate design consequence. In the
Gnome Mage penetration world and the Night Elf Druid mana world, the two
single-slot replacements and their additive forecast fit the selected cap,
while the actual joint replacement exceeds it in the local confirmation. The
Druid case also passes its one held-out setting. The Mage held-out joint is
instead supported safe at the same frozen cap, despite a positive interaction.
The model starts with only
`a00/x1`; other partners are future items. The original all-partner initial
library had no such missed-cap decision in the broad/refined checks. Changing
that initial publication state is an explicit model change using the same
development table, not another independent native observation.

The mechanisms have familiar explanations. Mage penetration saturates when
target resistance is removed, changing the value of spell power and sometimes
the sign of the interaction. Druid regeneration permits a different action
allocation, complementing damage per cast. The challenged Starfire mana/time
guard did not explain the Druid long-task interaction: its alternative guard
settings left those samples unchanged. Calling this “nonlinear MP5
regeneration” would be inaccurate because the direct regeneration contribution
is linear in the audited engine.

Signed reverse controls are useful here: redundant penetration and redundant
mana replacements reverse the interaction sign. Near-zero or nonsignificant
controls are not equivalence tests. These cases motivate checking the full
composition response and preserving source-specific witnesses. They do not
make interaction, saturation, resource coupling or a standard four-corner
contrast original scientific concepts.

The theoretical consequence is elementary but important for the update
contract: with permanent availability and immutable responses, a cap violation
persists in every later extension. A later repair can rescue an old source's
usefulness but cannot erase an over-cap joint configuration. This joins the
two candidate cores: useful repair opportunities themselves can be excluded
by composition safety. Developing a useful compressed interface for both
positive witnesses and forbidden compositions would be a stronger contribution
than another nonlinear example.

## Ideas the evidence rejects or leaves unestablished

| Idea | Judgment |
|---|---|
| More nonlinear/rank-changing tables imply an important new mechanism | Rejected as a criterion. Require a changed design decision, causal/source audit and explicit scope. |
| Exact planning broadly outperforms sensible simple baselines | Not supported. Strong minimum-increment and lookahead baselines explain almost all apparent advantages; remaining count gaps mostly stage the same terminal value. |
| The observed Shaman batch advantage survives realistic alternatives | Rejected in the restored finite menu. |
| The original Magister `.01` retention separation is stable | Rejected by N=4096 development. The `.005` candidate is a new hypothesis. |
| Nearly matched frontier plus an unlabeled scalar/source-mass summary settles continuation | Challenged by the independently confirmed full-domain Trinket example. Labeled response/incidence information distinguishes it. |
| All one-step DPS methods fail on Trinket | False for the tested full domain: equal-weight max-gain succeeds. |
| The Druid effect is caused by the challenged Starfire guard | Not supported by the executed policy challenge. |
| Source retention penalties automatically require a new theory | False. Many come from the old primary's immutable best use tightening available headroom. |
| All registered classes/races and all proposed branches were explored | False. Mechanism exploration covers seven classes; smoke/baseline coverage is different. New-task expansion was not executed. |
| Native repeated frontier-neutral behavioral growth or an arbitrarily long repair cascade was established here | Not established. These are separate supplied theoretical directions. |

## Independent confirmation and its failed transfer

The immutable manifest is `CONFIRMATION_MANIFEST_V2.json` under the campaign
artifact root. Eight setting-level claims spend total alpha `.05`, four
local/held-out pairs at `.0125` each. Catalog settings use N=131072 per physical
cell; joint stat-variant settings use N=32768. The earlier manifest version had
no native execution and no alpha expenditure. Local fresh-seed replication and
one unseen transformed physical setting per case are distinct outcomes; neither
is broad population transfer.

The frozen catalog claim requires supported first releases, simultaneous
frontier equivalence within `.0025`, retaining capacity separation at both
widths one and two, and equal exactly bounded value-only continuations. A
supported good path alone is insufficient. Joint claims require both isolated
replacements and the additive forecast safe, the actual joint unsafe, and a
positive mixed contrast. No result-dependent threshold or sample extension is
allowed after these tests. Approximate simultaneous paired Student-t inference
targets the pinned executable simulator, not live game behavior.

| Frozen finding | Local status | Held-out status | Current publication judgment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Actual Magister source-specific continuation, new `e=.005` | Confirmed: retaining `1 vs 2`, value `3 vs 3`, B1/B2 | Same closed bounds confirmed | Supporting case; simple task/source coverage explains the choice. |
| Actual Trinket source-specific continuation | Confirmed: retaining `0 vs 1`, value `1 vs 1`, B1/B2 | Same closed bounds confirmed | Recommended core example, with finite domain, unchanged physical crosses and successful simple baselines stated. |
| Mage joint cap missed by additive forecast, `h=.043` | Confirmed | **Not confirmed: actual joint supported safe** | A local composition-safety example with a real transfer boundary. |
| Druid joint cap missed by additive forecast, `h=.055` | Confirmed | Confirmed | Supporting resource/composition example, not a new regeneration law. |

The Mage held-out resistance75 cap-margin interval is
`[1.806975,2.625500]` DPS, entirely on the safe side. Its mixed interaction
interval remains `[6.666534,7.192440]` DPS, entirely positive. The difference
between a response interaction and a changed admission decision is therefore
observed directly. The cap was not retuned to convert this failed transfer
into a success. `CONFIRMED_CLAIMS.json` retains the failed prespecified
condition; `analysis/confirm-v2-results/CLAIM_06.json` retains its intervals.

## What would justify a new paper core

A defensible next paper would center on a **decision-preserving representation
of update opportunities under source retention and composition safety**. The
finite native cases can motivate and test it. The theoretical work must add a
useful characterization, compression or certified method beyond existing
abstraction/interface/coverage tools, and the evidence must show why it changes
a meaningful decision after strong baselines and same-budget comparisons.
The present campaign has not yet supplied that new theorem or a novelty claim.

The confirmed finite claims already justify a focused application/measurement
result with an audited benchmark and substantial negative findings. A
foundational new-paper claim still needs the theoretical delta and a deeper
prior-work comparison. Preserve the Mage local result together with its failed
transfer. The recommendation is to build around **future source witnesses
blocked by immutable composition**, using Trinket as the clearest finite native
example and Magister/joint-cap cases as support, rather than making a new
algorithm or universal sustainability claim.
