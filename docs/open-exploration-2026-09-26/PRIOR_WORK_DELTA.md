## Conditions for promoting a finding

1. Preserve a small complete native instance with exact release/source incidence and a decision that differs from a reasonable baseline.
2. Keep the baseline's permissions and information equal; separately label information ablation or increased coordination freedom.
3. Challenge missing weak/alternative repairs, task weighting, policy reoptimization, realistic parameter bounds and threshold sensitivity.
4. Confirm the finite claim on independent seeds after freezing the full comparison family and error allocation. Hold out worlds separately if claiming transfer.
5. Map any proposed theorem/algorithm to existing state abstraction, coverage/ordering and interface variables. A proof plus a finite native case still does not establish published priority.

## Frozen confirmation changes the evidence, not the novelty standard

The final v2 confirmation supports both untouched-catalog continuation cases locally and in their one generated held-out setting. At B=1 and B=2, Magister's forced first choices have retained continuation [1,1] versus [2,2] with value-only [3,3] on both sides; the trinket/offhand choices have [0,0] versus [1,1] with value-only [1,1] on both sides. Both first releases are feasible and their frontiers meet the predeclared equivalence margin on every task. These are conditional approximate-t results under the fixed complete menus, thresholds, source definitions and native policies. They support a design warning about discarding source/task identity even when first performance is matched. They do not show that existing richer interfaces or a simple source-coverage baseline fail.

The mana-regeneration individual/additive-versus-joint cap discrepancy also survives its held-out setting. The penetration discrepancy is local only: its held-out interaction remains positive, but the selected joint configuration is supported cap-safe. This failure is useful evidence that nonzero interaction is not a transferable admission-error claim. The four local cases answer two decision questions; they are not four new mechanisms, and seven passing records are not seven independent discoveries.

The strongest application candidate is therefore the actual-equipment continuation pair with complete-menu, equal-value-only and held-out checks. A new paper would still need a contribution beyond a scalar information-ablation counterexample and a native demonstration of known task coverage. The joint-budget cases are complementary evidence about composition checks, with the failed transfer kept visible. Exact subset search, paired t bounds, union bounds and the compact sufficient-moment archive remain standard tools rather than originality claims.

No new manuscript was written and no review system was queried in this audit. Primary-source novelty remains unresolved; the evidence can motivate a focused new-paper question without asserting priority.
