## Legality and fidelity boundaries

The faction audit finds eight strict UI faction-tag conflicts among the 96 broad registry worlds, involving the Alliance-tagged Songstone of Ironforge (12543) in Horde Druid/Warlock presets. Ten Human Rogue worlds contain faction-named PvP pieces (23258 and 22879) with no encoded faction restriction, leaving acquisition/equip semantics unresolved. The engine's physical Item/SimItem path does not enforce the UI faction tag; the UI also uses it as a filter and database generation includes acquisition-derived tags. Therefore the executed numeric result is real for this executable, while a claim of verified faction-obtainable equipment would be unwarranted. These are not grounds for inventing native execution failures.

The actual-item catalog replaces those preset items with explicitly recorded neutral items and has no encoded faction conflict. Those replacements change stats/set effects and are new worlds, not retroactive fixes to frozen physics. Actual-catalog items have empty parameter overrides; the research variants use real base IDs with altered stats/effect amplitudes and remain distinct from attainable items. Native-pool inclusion does not independently certify live acquisition, phase availability or complete server behavior.

The source audit rejects the Classic damage-to-rage explanation: Forever rage per white swing uses base weapon speed, while crit can act indirectly through Flurry. Universal hit/crit pooling, Thunderfury's zero-damage bounce, Life Tap's omitted self-damage outside tanking, and pet/racial omissions restrict interpretations. This is a stationary DPS platform with no incoming attacks; no healing, survival, full PvP or live-economy conclusion follows.

## Statistical and novelty boundaries for the review package

The development thresholds, first pairs, initial libraries and deep-challenge sample sizes were selected adaptively. Fresh seeds used during that adaptive phase are still development data. They do not become confirmation because their N is large. Full-menu finite exactness is combinatorial exactness on the measured means with the reported numerical tolerance; it is not an exact population result.

The independent inference review checks simultaneous paired Student-t bounds, reference covariance, max/min directions, every published source, retained bad-cap cells, matching equivalence and optimistic upper-graph searches. Student-t coverage is approximate and fixed-N. A completed lower graph alone is never an optimum certificate; a timed-out upper graph retains a coarse valid upper bound. Controls share a preallocated family, and nonsignificance is not equivalence. Raw simulation seeds and sample order are audited separately from array shape.

The v2 plan reserves alpha .00625 for each of eight local/held-out records, total .05. The four local settings concern two actual-equipment continuation cases and two joint-budget cases; they are not four independent new mechanisms. Within each record, core and auxiliary families remain within that allocation. The actual manifest passed independent preflight before any confirmation batch/receipt existed: full domains, positive development-disjoint and mutually disjoint seeds, physical references, N, thresholds, current analysis hashes, adjudication rules and held-out generation all matched. `checks/CONFIRMATION_PREFLIGHT_V2.json` records the checks. This is design approval, not support for any resulting scientific claim.

The first confirmation manifest was superseded without native sampling to add adjudication identity/provenance guards. `CONFIRMATION_V1_NOT_RUN.json` records zero calls and zero alpha expenditure; v2 preserves the exact same scientific design, physical jobs and seeds. Both manifests remain available. The final v2 batch completed 960 logical cells and 72,351,744 battles at fixed N=131072 for actual-catalog records and N=32768 for joint records. These completed requests are included in the final campaign totals at the beginning of this audit and excluded from the explicitly pre-confirmation table.

The useful candidate question is whether a first-release summary preserves enough task/source information for later admissibility, and whether individual/additive power checks preserve joint safety. Known task-coverage, richer quality/resource interfaces, exact finite-state planning and simultaneous inference directly apply. Their use is not a new method. The R5 least-closure result and old scalar structural results remain separate formal inputs, not new native coverage. No claim of priority follows from the supplied reviews or from a native counterexample. `PRIOR_WORK_DELTA.md` records the primary-source comparisons and the remaining novelty uncertainty.
