## Checked mathematical directions

`oe_inference.FiniteBounds` constructs all ordered configuration-pair contrasts for gain and retention, plus every configuration's power contrast. For C configurations and Q tasks, the declared family size `Q*(2*C*C+C)` is conservative even if offsets happen to coincide. The two-sided critical value is `t.isf(alpha/(2*family_size), N-1)`. Every contrast includes the fresh physical reference vector and its covariance. The arithmetic therefore targets differences of population means against the mean of a fixed physical reference, not a randomly reselected confirmation winner or a noiseless development mean.

For sets A and B, the target `max_A(mu)-max_B(mu)-offset*S` equals both `max_a min_b d_ab` and `min_b max_a d_ab`. Applying the lower intervals to the former and upper intervals to the latter gives correctly directed bounds. This covers source-best-versus-frontier and gain comparisons over the same frozen finite domain. The source incidence is intersected with the actual published configuration set. Power checks include every active legal configuration; a bad cell is not silently removed.

Task-mass support uses only tasks whose lower margin passes. Optimistic task mass uses every task whose upper margin has not failed. Union-bound validity does not require independence across tasks/configurations; the t approximation assumes suitable independent observations across seed indices. Common random streams within each seed are retained. Missing cells, invalid weights and unbudgeted offsets are rejected.

## Graph interpretation

`oe_confidence_planner` forms states from the full actual published subset, including old and newly introduced source obligations. Its lower graph gives feasible-history lower bounds. Its upper graph is an optimistic relaxation: only a completed search supplies its finite capacity upper bound; a timed-out upper search retains a coarse remaining-item bound. An optimistic incumbent is not treated as a positive lower bound. No point-mean cap rejection or headroom pruning removes candidates from the upper graph. Continuation preserves the original items and charges already released items against the total item budget.

The inference primitives do not establish sample provenance, a complete candidate menu, physical legality, or the correctness of source incidence; the confirmation assembler must verify them. They also do not certify a continuous domain or exact distribution-free coverage. The supplied paired Student-t method is explicitly approximate. A zero empirical variance yields a point interval, which is an arithmetic consequence, not proof of native determinism.

## Required caller checks before confirmation

- Freeze the entire physical configuration/task/policy domain and order, plus the mapping from each task to its development-selected old reference configuration. Remeasure that identity using confirmation seeds; do not pick a fresh maximum.
- Confirm equal seed identities/order and complete fixed N across paired configurations. Shape equality alone is insufficient. Reject duplicate seed observations as extra samples, and check independence from development at the exact physical-design/seed level.
- Sum all claim/world/core/auxiliary allocations over the campaign. Reusing one simultaneous family for B choices and histories is allowed. New offsets, thresholds or extra auxiliary contrast families require their preallocated coverage; a failed claim does not return spent alpha.
- A first-step difference within .0025 based on development means is only a screening match. A matched-first claim needs simultaneous upper bounds for both `F_A-F_B-.0025*S` and `F_B-F_A-.0025*S`, no larger than zero on every required task.
- For a joint-equipment failure claim, an auxiliary family should distinguish initial/individual power safety, additive-prediction safety, and a strictly positive native joint cap violation. Include all covariance terms in `U11-U10-U01+U00` or the corresponding additive cap margin. An interaction above zero alone does not prove a changed admissibility decision.
- Initial validity must be confidence-supported for a positive feasible-history claim; otherwise mark unresolved. Keep finite-table exact means, supported paths and optimistic population upper bounds in separate fields.
- Index thinning in `oe_planning` is documented as an exploratory finite subdomain. A confirmation upper bound applies to the explicitly frozen domain, not to deleted or unobserved candidate repairs.

## Confirmation assembler review

`oe_confirmation` constructs every Cartesian candidate × partner × task × policy cell in the declared frozen domain; it rejects missing, extra, duplicate, reconstructed, nonfinite and unequal-N cells. Policies are configurations, not silently optimized response values. Each task's old reference is an explicit physical configuration; its fresh samples enter the paired contrasts. The assembler does not reselect that reference using confirmation means. An optional frozen input hash must match when supplied, so the actual scientific manifest must supply it.

The batch-level checks now compare the frozen protocol's canonical job hash to JOBS, compare every observed row's identity and physical input hash to its job, and compare its native binary identity to the protocol. Each claim must freeze a seed block, or a start seed and N, and the observed allocation must match. Registry initial-history and task-weight differences require explicit reasons. A manifest with only job-derived domains is marked with that narrower source; a capacity claim about the original menu requires an explicit complete world domain in the manifest. These checks were added in response to this independent review before any confirmation data were supplied.

The campaign allocator adds every declared claim alpha to previous expenditure and compares the sum with a nonrenewable budget no greater than .05. Each claim reserves half for the core finite family and half for auxiliaries. If both matched-first and joint diagnostics are requested, each gets one quarter; if neither is requested, the unused half remains reserved. Reusing a core family for several release widths and histories does not add new contrasts. Core path checks separately preserve initial-state validity, every subsequent prefix, item budgets and all newly released source obligations. Search timeouts remain unresolved with the appropriate coarse upper bound.

The matched-first auxiliary correctly uses a separate family at the predeclared equivalence margin and requires the upper endpoint of each directional contrast to be nonpositive on every task. It retains the unselected alternative in both continuation pools and separately reports whether each first update was feasible. Therefore a finite upper bound on one continuation and a supported lower bound on the other can show a capacity gap only when those additional prefix and matching checks also pass.

The subsequently added value-only continuations repeat that same domain with only the source-retention requirement removed. They preserve initial power validity, universal power checks over every active physical configuration, gain, monotone history and the remaining item budget. Internal value-only nodes skip unused source arithmetic; initial and returned-path records still retain every source diagnostic with `retention_enforced=false`. This is an objective relaxation using the already covered power/gain contrasts, not a new statistical family. The old/source-aware continuation remains separately available.

The joint auxiliary is limited to a frozen policy and exactly one old item per slot. It requires the reference identity to equal the four-cell old baseline. Its five contrasts per task are individual cap margins, the joint cap margin, the additive forecast cap margin and the mixed difference. The signs are correct: individual/forecast safety needs a nonnegative lower endpoint; a joint cap violation needs a negative upper endpoint; positive interaction needs a positive lower endpoint. This is a decision discrepancy only when the safety and violation conditions jointly hold. Other policies/configurations and all-source retention remain separate core checks.

Optional predeclared control pairs enlarge this one auxiliary family to `5*Q*(1+number_of_controls)`. The common critical value uses the full enlarged family, so showing the same family alpha on each control is not a separate spending allocation. An inconclusive control interaction is explicitly not an equivalence finding.

The assembler intentionally leaves `claim_supported` unset: successful assembly is not scientific confirmation. The separate frozen adjudicator requires all prespecified conditions jointly. Matched-first cases require both first releases supported, simultaneous frontier equivalence, a retaining-capacity separation for every declared B, and closed equal value-only capacity bounds. Joint cases require individual safety, additive safety, joint exclusion and positive mixed interaction. It verifies the exact analysis-frozen manifest, claim/world identities and each claim's canonical hash before deciding. A record must contain exactly one claim type. A failed support test remains not-confirmed, which can mean excluded or unresolved. No inference or planner code was changed by this reviewer.

## Native sample ordering

The native bridge invokes `core.RunRaidSim`, which uses the serial `RunSim` path. The engine initializes the random generator at the requested seed and resets it to `seed+i` before each later iteration; labeled streams are reset with that same iteration seed. `DistributionMetrics.doneIteration` appends each sample in order and `ToProto` exposes that vector directly as `allValues`. The summarizer retains that order. A positive start seed is required: engine seed zero substitutes clock time on the first iteration. The actual frozen jobs must also retain `saveAllValues` and labeled random streams.

The independent development allocation scan in `checks/DEVELOPMENT_SEED_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.json` records every declared non-confirmation JOBS seed interval, including requests that may not have succeeded. A disjoint confirmation interval is therefore a conservative fresh-seed check. Sharing a seed across physical cells inside one confirmation allocation is the intended paired design, not repeated independent sample evidence.
