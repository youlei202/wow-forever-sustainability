# Prior work, formal provenance, and the remaining contribution

Primary sources below were reopened on 25 September 2026. This targeted check
does not establish that the specialized completion-capacity identity is novel.
The paper should present its corrected conditional statement, native transfer
test, and failure cases without claiming a new general theory of Pareto
dominance, interval packing, transfer learning, or automated game balancing.

## The supplied R5 theory is a different formal basis

The user-supplied R5 note is retained verbatim under
`WOWFS_WORK_ROOT/inputs/r5-theory/THEORY.md`, SHA-256
`a2160fda7467f3618797c91f2257f6dac39b284804774cf0751eac1d92cd8dd2`.
It proves a least repair closure under unique source-specific repairs, a
restricted cascade construction, and a frontier-neutral behavioral packing
lower bound. Its neutral theorem explicitly preserves the old utility frontier;
it does not require strictly positive task-value gain per update.

The new brief's completion-gap formula is not contained in that R5 note and
does not follow from its theorems. The current [THEORY.md](THEORY.md) derives a
separate scalar completion model with positive gains. Its open-band formula
requires a continuous accessible incoming family and complete additive mixing.
A closed minimum design amplitude can truncate the attainable band or add a
direct branch; open and closed endpoints change integer capacities. Full
source relevance can further reduce capacity. The maintained counterexamples
and exact rational tests retain all of these distinctions.

| Formal object | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Supplied R5 repair closure | Minimum completion of a specified core under unique repair and stable-witness assumptions | General multitask repair, interchangeable repairs, or the completion-gap formula |
| Supplied R5 frontier-neutral region | A finite lower bound on useful behaviorally distinct releases with unchanged task frontiers | Strictly positive utility gain per update |
| New accessible scalar response bands | Exact value capacity under a declared amplitude domain, endpoint conventions, and complete cap-safe mixing | Automatic old/new source relevance or native behavioral novelty |
| New retained-source constructions | Particular matched five-partner examples and a fixed-budget recovery with all source witnesses checked | A universal legacy-preserving escape or an original behavioral-D guarantee |
| Native fixed-control validation | Agreement of specified native seeds/design points with the scalar response reduction | Global exact affinity, population-exact Monte Carlo means, or every possible player policy |

The proposed native distinction is quantitative and falsifiable: equal initial
frontiers, task caps, source counts, source-use masses, and small portfolios can
coexist with different later capacities in the same incoming design family.
The old suboptimal-response distributions are deliberately different and must
be shown, rather than described as identical ecologies. Source-preserving
recovery must report its admission cost and reduced immediate opportunity.
Successful scalar value/retention sequences do not establish the original
seven-condition behavioral objective.

The strongest admissible originality wording is therefore a model-specific
conditional characterization with a native test, accompanied by a statement
that exhaustive novelty has not been established. Three native event/control
families provide transfer evidence only if their separately frozen capacity
experiments succeed; three classes varying an ordinary stat alone do not
establish mechanism diversity.
