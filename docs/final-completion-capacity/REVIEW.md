# Final completion-capacity review

1. **Can identical current value coexist with different future capacity?** Yes, within the validated scalar-response scope. The 56 pairs of old ecologies have exactly identical taskwise optima, 5 partners each, 100% legacy-source relevance, and K=1. In 51 combinations, large-gap ecologies support 4 releases while dense ecologies have a conditional continuous upper bound of 2; the prespecified robust dense path actually supports 1 release.
2. **Are the exact theoretical predictions correct?** The conditional direction is supported. Frozen predictions are 5 for large-gap, 2 for dense, and 5 for weak direct design; the budget prediction is 4 for 38 combinations and 3 for 13. Native confirmation does not measure exactly 5 versus 2: endpoint paths retain unresolved thresholds and failures. The formula must specify reachable intervals, open/closed endpoints, minimum amplitudes, and legacy-source constraints.
3. **Does weak direct design restore capacity?** A prospective release policy starting from the same old library supports 4 releases, exceeding the dense family's conditional upper bound of 2. This does not mean that already released dense histories can refund consumed headroom; remaining releases after saturation of the original history are still limited by remaining headroom.
4. **Does a fixed compatibility/resource rule restore capacity?** Yes: on the same strong-primary family, one fixed two-feature linear budget supports 3 releases, retains every old combination, and uses zero per-item exceptions. The rule is a research admission budget, not a game-native mana mechanism in disguise.
5. **What current opportunities are sacrificed?** In the 51 valid mapping combinations, the three-release robust path excludes 3–6 new combinations that would otherwise pass frozen power admission; the maximum first-release task opportunity loss is 2.16%–3.27% of the old reference value, with zero old combinations excluded. The exact nominal model's four-release budget path reduces the reachable peak by 0.00975.
6. **Does it hold across mechanisms?** Universal support was not obtained. Independent native confirmation is complete for weapon speed, mana regeneration, and proc damage over time, each in one Alliance and one Horde context; no nonempty prespecified path simultaneously passes all P/G/N/L interval tests. Finite mean capacities, directional counterexamples, and original/subsequent sensitivity analyses are all preserved.
7. **Is it robust across Alliance/Horde?** In the 26 Alliance and 25 Horde combinations with valid scalar mappings, the supported 4/1/4/3 paths all reproduce. The main faction table weights classes equally while retaining finite experiments for all 5 Warlock contexts; prediction columns contain only the 8 applicable classes, observation columns contain all 9, and denominators are explicit.
8. **How many classes and race/class combinations are complete?** All 9/9 classes and 56/56 official combinations have actual execution; 51/56 validate the scalar mapping, while 5/56 Warlock contexts do not admit the formula. Known racial-mechanism omissions affect 7 combinations and are marked. Warlock's 20 exact endpoint paths are not_run and never filled with zeros; all of its robust cross-combinations were executed.
9. **What is the strongest defensible main claim?** Within the specified native equipment/policy families and validated affine-response model, identical current optima, legacy-source relevance, and management complexity do not determine future value-retaining capacity at fixed resolution; reachable completion intervals and the minimum direct-design scale provide testable restrictions and costly prospective interventions.
10. **Is this sufficient to anchor an AISTATS manuscript?** It supports a complete research manuscript centered on those conditional results and failure boundaries, but does not establish a generic cross-mechanism law, complete seven-condition sustainable expansion, mathematical originality, or guaranteed Oral-level quality. The anonymous paper is written around the actual evidence.

## Evidence and limits

The study executed **22,032 native calls and 8,181,632 battles**, with zero failed or missing physical cells. Exact native design-seed observations are not duplicated. Shared seed ranges across different physical designs are disclosed; combat seeds are not independent research units.

All 51 affine contexts pass the guarded P/N/L/H/C/G paths at the mean and under approximate simultaneous paired Student-t bounds **within each context**. This is not a joint 95% claim over all 56 contexts, nor a distribution-free or live-server guarantee. The continuous dense upper bound reuses the same contrast family, so it adds no separate unbudgeted testing family. Its validity remains conditional on the affine model across the declared family.

Original Warrior behavioral D fails at the mean in all 274 attempted stages. Other classes' physical-channel diagnostic does not substitute for the original seven coordinates. No fully certified T_joint sequence is claimed.

The finite candidate-union oracle gives mean capacities 4/1/4 for large/dense/direct in all 51 applicable contexts; budget is 4 in 33 and 3 in 18. It allows arbitrary release order and retains all historical crosses/sources. Its finite mean optimum is not a continuous or confidence-certified optimum. Generic models with the same two features contain the linear budget; no algorithmic advantage is claimed.
