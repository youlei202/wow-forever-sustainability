# The tolerance contract is a design variable

The pre-confirmation secondary protocol evaluates every one of the 128 registered base-menu queries (16 catalogues ×8 queries) at source-retention tolerances 0.5%, 1%, 2%, and 5% of the original reference response. All other power, gain, task-mass and physical-menu conditions remain fixed. These 512 rows are nested deterministic consequences of the same fresh events; they are not independent observations or an estimate of population prevalence.

| Retention tolerance | Certified YES | Certified NO | UNKNOWN | Retention-essential NO | Catalogues with such NO |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5% | 21 | 92 | 15 | 81 | 15 |
| 1% | 42 | 76 | 10 | 69 | 15 |
| 2% | 68 | 50 | 10 | 45 | 11 |
| 5% | 106 | 14 | 8 | 11 | 4 |

“Retention-essential NO” means that full completion is certified impossible while the same query with all source-retention obligations removed has a conservative YES witness. It does not establish which particular source group causes the obstruction; that question needs the separate obligation audit.

At 2% tolerance, 20 of the original 76 certified NO queries acquire certified completions; at 5%, 58 do. The source-retention tolerance therefore materially changes feasible update design. A claim that the observed blockage is insensitive to the normative tolerance would be false. Conversely, 11 retention-essential certified NO queries across four catalogues survive at 5%; it would also be false that a moderately relaxed tolerance always eliminates source obligations as a constraint.

At 5%, all 32 mage queries have certified completions. Druid, paladin, and warrior strata respectively have YES/NO/UNKNOWN counts 30/2/0, 27/5/0, and 17/7/8. The eight unresolved warrior queries remain UNKNOWN, not negative outcomes.

The calculation inherits the original event: its diagonal retention and gain contrasts imply intervals for the reference response; changing the tolerance shifts retention bounds by the sign-aware interval multiple of that same reference. No fresh critical value, sample, or alpha allocation is introduced. Cap and gain arrays are unchanged. The frozen analysis hashes, original compact moments, original bounds, fixed N and seed blocks are verified before computation. The original fixed-N paired-t approximation remains the statistical limitation.

Results and provenance are in `native/secondary-tolerance-v1/`, including `SUMMARY.json`, all 512 CSV/JSON decisions, and 16 per-catalogue `TOLERANCE_RESULTS.json` files. The portable checker independently recomputes exact mean answers with its rational mask oracle and replays each confidence certificate from the original moments.
