# Targeted precedent check for C/D/E

This is a narrow check prompted by the native screening results. It is not an
originality certification. Generic threshold filters, overlap-aware coverage,
minimum supports, and complementarities already have substantial literatures.

| Target hypothesis | Primary precedent inspected | Consequence for an R4 claim |
|---|---|---|
| Withholding an offered competitor can preserve another option's useful role. | Wang, Zhao and Ke, *Modeling Consumer Choice and Optimizing Assortment under the Threshold Multinomial Logit Model*, author abstract: low-utility alternatives can be excluded by dominance from another offered alternative. [Author manuscript record](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/SSRN_ID4190089_code1318674.pdf?abstractid=4184044&mirid=1). | The threshold-cannibalization idea is established. Our exact witness-frontier formulation must be compared with this literature; the fact that all cap-safe options need not jointly preserve usefulness is not itself a foundational novelty. The abstract was retrieved; the canonical SSRN page failed to open, so no claim about an unseen theorem is made. |
| A small conjunction of threshold rules describes an admissible offer set. | Aouad, Farias and Levi, *Assortment Optimization Under Consider-then-Choose Choice Models*, author manuscript, §2 and later structural algorithms. It explicitly treats conjunctive, disjunctive and compensatory screening rules. [Author PDF](https://web.mit.edu/~vivekf/www/papers/MS-Static.pdf). | Response boxes are an established representation class. R4's conditional closure statement packages its own joint witnesses; it does not invent conjunctive admission or prove a generic-versus-structured separation. |
| A useful extension may require a bundle rather than an individually useful item. | Immorlica, Lucier, Mao, Syrgkanis and Tzamos, *Combinatorial Assortment Optimization*. Customers can choose bundles and valuations include complements/substitutes. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.02601). | Bundle choice is already a formal subject. Native minimum release size two must come with a mechanism, retained old obligations and an unseen prediction, not merely renamed bundle complementarity. |
| The number of items needed together is a structural measure of complementarity. | Feige and Izsak, *Welfare Maximization and the Supermodular Degree*, introduces a quantitative measure of complementarity. [Author PDF](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~feige/mypapers/SupermodularDegree.pdf). Chen, Teng and Zhang introduce complementarity witness measures including supermodular width. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.04436). | Do not call a support size or hyperedge rank a new complementarity theory. The current joint-feasibility predicate also differs from a monotone welfare function: newly released items add usefulness obligations, so enlarging the release can destroy joint feasibility. A monotone D-support subproblem is only one component. |
| Several protected sources can share one configuration and one demand condition. | Bredereck et al., *Multiwinner Elections With Diversity Constraints*, considers performance and candidate attributes jointly. [AAAI paper record](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/11457). | Attribute coverage and representation constraints are established. No one-to-one source/task matching should be imposed without a separate exclusivity assumption. Endogenous competitiveness and H/archive obligations would need separate analysis. |
| Individual components can need a compatible joint installation. | The EDOS project's [formalization](https://www.mancoosi.org/edos/formalization/), Definitions 3–6, distinguishes healthy installations, installability, co-installability and dependency closure. It models conjunctive/disjunctive dependencies and conflicts. | Minimum feasible batches and SAT encodings are not new logical concepts. Our empirical usefulness/behavior predicates must add a substantive domain result; item-release syntax alone cannot support novelty. EDOS explicitly notes that individually installable packages need not be co-installable, a different obstruction direction from the observed pair-only D witnesses. |
| Hyperedge size measures how many goods must occur together. | Abraham, Babaioff, Dughmi and Roughgarden, [*Combinatorial Auctions with Restricted Complements*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.4104), represents valuations by weighted hypergraphs and uses hyperedge size as degree of complementarity. | A qualifying-support hypergraph and its rank are diagnostic representations, not newly introduced measures. Our complete feasibility predicate is not asserted to be a weighted-hypergraph valuation. |
| Repeated successful actions require a state from which continuation remains possible. | Aubin, Lygeros, Quincampoix, Sastry and Seube, [*Viability and Invariance Kernels of Impulse Differential Inclusions*](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sastry/pubs/PDFs%20of%20Pubs2000-2005/Pdfs%20of%20Misc.Others/Aubin/AubinViability.pdf), formulates constrained trajectories and the largest viable/invariant subsets. | Naming the finite backward predecessor recursion a viability kernel is established mathematics. The finite release state must include protected choices, archive and useful-source registry; equal current frontiers do not imply equal continuation states. A native counterexample can identify missing state, but does not invent viability theory. |

The immediate research priority remains causal native separation and an
independent prediction. The mathematical results currently serve as precise
diagnostics and constructive certificates. If a selected mechanism survives
confirmation, its new contribution must be stated relative to these existing
objects rather than advertised as a new threshold, coverage, or complementarity
formalism.
