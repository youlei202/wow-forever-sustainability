# Closest primary work and the remaining delta

These papers were checked directly during the decisive-value study. The list
does not certify exhaustive novelty. Mathematical tools below are credited as
background. Cached native screens are development evidence; the separately
frozen three-update candidate also has independent confirmation data, with
the inference limits stated below.

| Primary work | Relevant existing result or method | What this study must add, rather than rename |
|---|---|---|
| Roijers, Whiteson and Oliehoek, [Computing Convex Coverage Sets for Faster Multi-objective Coordination](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/article/view/10933), JAIR2015 | Convex coverage sets, scalarized support searches and factored coordination allow useful-option search without retaining every Pareto point. | A physically supported fixed decision family and actual old-library reoptimization. Adding a new supported performance vector alone is not a new result. Coupled caps must be included before interpreting component replacement. |
| Alegre, Bazzan and da Silva, [Optimistic Linear Support and Successor Features as a Basis for Optimal Policy Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11326), ICML2022 | Successor features with linear rewards connect transfer to multiobjective coverage; policy improvement can exploit a learned coverage set. | Native shared resource/control changes cannot be assumed to preserve fixed successor features. Old and new libraries receive identical policy permissions; fixed channel differences are not themselves new tasks. |
| Budijono et al., [Ludus](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/21550), AAAI2022 | Automated playtesting, sampled approximations and global parameter search optimize metagame balance. | Fixed historical-source obligations, complete new/old coequipment and repeated separately positive decision gain would need an explicit joint structural characterization. Simulation-based balancing or parameter optimization is already established. |
| Mouret and Clune, [Illuminating search spaces by mapping elites](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04909), 2015 | Search can preserve high-quality solutions across behavior descriptors. | Our independent G requirement distinguishes behavioral diversity from additional achievable utility under declared combat decisions. A descriptor-distance success cannot substitute for G. |
| EDOS/MANCOOSI, [Formalization of package relations](https://www.mancoosi.org/edos/formalization/) | Dependencies, conflicts and co-installability formalize cases where individual installability differs from joint installability; requirements may be shared or disjunctive. | A minimal batch or support hypergraph alone is not new. Our batch condition must arise from quantitative native utility/cap/retention responses and produce positive G after old-library reoptimization. |
| Ahmadi, Raith and Jalili, [A Fast and Simple Algorithm for the Resource Constrained Shortest Path Problem](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol351-esa2025/html/LIPIcs.ESA.2025.97/LIPIcs.ESA.2025.97.html), ESA2025, Definition2 | Dominance requires both favorable objective cost and no greater resource use. Its validity preserves possible feasible continuations. | A reward-weaker component becoming essential under an upper capability cap is consistent with known constrained dominance. Reward-only component pruning omitted a relevant constraint direction. That observation is a mechanism diagnosis, not a new dominance theorem. |
| Geilen, Basten, Theelen and Otten, [An Algebra of Pareto Points](https://tbasten.estue.nl/papers/pareto.pdf), 2007, Section6.4 Definition16, Proposition10 and Corollary11 | Pruning before component composition is valid under constraints closed under dominating replacements. | Our cap on maximized utility violates that stated condition. Current coverage failing as a future-completion summary is an instance outside its premises, not a refutation or new qualitative discovery. |
| Hendriks et al., [Interface Modeling for Quality and Resource Management](https://lmcs.episciences.org/7513/pdf), LMCS2021, Definition3.19, Proposition3.20 and Theorem5.2 | Component interface refinement composes through Pareto operations with appropriately safe constraints. | A useful new specialization would quantify simultaneous historical-source retention and positive-G extension at fixed resolution. Its originality remains unestablished; resource/interface modeling itself is existing work. |

Our current exact tools—support-function screening, additive source-gap
cancellation, capped completion intervals, shared permanent source witnesses,
finite-table dynamic programming and a telescoping gain budget—do not yet
provide an established nontrivial theoretical novelty claim. The additional
abstract sequence class proves all seven goals jointly, and the finite-resolution
summary example quantifies a gap between current and extension-sufficient
coverage while preserving old sources. Neither has an originality certificate.
A complete
contribution would require a class-level relation between primitive composition,
shared retention obligations, fixed positive gain and finite rule/portfolio
burden that predicts unseen native releases. The decisive-value construction
now has three empirical seven-condition passes in independent native data,
but its first1.0138% gain remains unresolved against the fixed1% threshold
under the reported simultaneous intervals. Original behavior distance has
no sampling-confidence certificate. These results do not supply an
originality certificate or a population guarantee for the whole sequence.

The current native distinction is concrete: selected weak-partner/strong-item
pairs create cap-feasible improvements over every old gear/policy choice,
while each singleton cannot reach the fixed gain threshold under any safe
admission. The cleaner weapon pair exhibits this without a large positive
fixed-policy mixed interaction. This is more specific than behavior-only
diversity, but it is not yet proof that the mathematical phenomenon is new.
Optimized generic one-row budgets match the selected arbitrary-admission
feasibility, so no structured-method performance advantage is claimed.
The permanent-source witness condition is sufficient for eight of ten final
native source aliases in the empirical table; OH1 and OH3 fall short of that
condition while still passing finite-horizon relevance. Thus the native
sequence is not an empirical realization of an all-source perpetual-retention
theorem.
